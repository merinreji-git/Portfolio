01 · Executive Summary

A practice with
real strengths — and a
digital presence that wasn't showing them

A Dublin-based psychotherapy and counselling practice offering individual, couples, family, and workplace wellness services. Skilled therapist network. Online counselling capability. A genuinely compassionate, client-centred offering. And a digital footprint that was effectively invisible.

The audit revealed a Domain Authority of just 11 — against 26 for the leading competitor. Only 7 ranking keywords. A backlink profile 163 times weaker than the top competitor. No active social media presence. Zero top-10 rankings for any high-value therapy search in Ireland.

In a sector where someone searching "therapy Dublin" at 11pm is often in genuine need, not being found is not a neutral outcome. The strategy I developed focused on closing that gap — technically, editorially, and socially — within six months.

Critical gap: The Organisation ranked for only 7 keywords versus 571 for Competitor A. Its backlink profile was 163× weaker. No top-10 rankings for any high-value therapy keyword — while two direct competitors ran active, well-optimised digital presences targeting the same audience.

🔍

DA of 11 vs 26

105 vs 17,200 backlinks

Technical SEO issues

Poor mobile page speed, broken 404 links, duplicate meta titles, missing schema markup, and incomplete GDPR compliance — all reducing search visibility.

📝

No content programme

No blog, no FAQ pages, no resource guides. Competitors were capturing informational search intent — the top of the funnel where trust is first built.

🌱

Strong upside potential

Rising mental health awareness, post-pandemic destigmatisation, and growing demand especially among 18–40s — the market was growing into a gap the practice could own.

04 · SWOT Analysis

An honest look at
where things stood

Strengths

  • Strong therapist network and personalised service
  • Diverse, client-centred therapy modalities
  • Online counselling capability already in place
  • Well-defined niche and established brand name

Weaknesses

  • Very low Domain Authority (DA 11)
  • Minimal backlink profile (105 links)
  • No top-10 keyword rankings
  • Limited social media presence
  • Slow page speed on mobile

Opportunities

  • Growing mental health awareness and demand post-pandemic
  • Corporate and workplace wellness programmes
  • Underserved long-tail keyword opportunities
  • International and expat online therapy market

Threats

  • Dominant competitors with established SEO authority
  • Regulatory changes — accreditation and data privacy
  • Increasing teletherapy platform competition
  • Reputational risk from unmanaged reviews

05 · Strategy

A six-month roadmap
built to close the gap

01

Fix the foundations

On-page SEO optimisation. Meta titles and descriptions. Fix 404 errors. Add schema markup. Improve mobile page speed. GDPR compliance. Month 1.

02

Build authority

Guest post outreach to 10+ mental health blogs. Directory submissions to 5+ high-DA Irish directories. Reclaim lost backlinks. Internal link optimisation. Months 1–3.

03

Create and publish

Launch structured blog programme. Service page optimisation. FAQ pages for featured snippets. Anonymised client stories for E-E-A-T. Resource guides to earn backlinks. Months 1–6.

04

Activate social

Facebook and Instagram activated with consistent, value-driven content. LinkedIn for corporate wellness targeting. Four-week content calendar with clear themes and formats. Months 1–6.

07 · Audience

Two people.
Two very different journeys
to the same door.

The Stressed Professional

28–42 · Mid-senior professional · Dublin · High digital usage

Pain Points

Work-life balance stress, anxiety and burnout, lack of time, stigma around therapy

Goals

Reduce anxiety, improve performance, structured CBT, discreet and convenient sessions

Channels

Google Search, LinkedIn, workplace wellness portals, online booking

Message

Evidence-based, professional, flexible — therapy that fits around a demanding life

25–38 · New parent · Dublin / Suburban · Moderate-high digital

Pain Points

Goals

Emotional support, mindfulness-based therapy, flexible or online sessions, affordable options

Channels

Facebook, Instagram, parenting forums, Google Search, low-cost counselling pages

Message

Compassionate, accessible, non-judgmental — you don't have to do this alone
