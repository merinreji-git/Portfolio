01 · Executive Summary

A brand with an
exceptional story
and an underutilised stage

A family-owned artisan ice cream brand founded in 2000 in a Gaeltacht town on the west coast of Kerry. Rare Kerry cow milk. Dingle Bay sea salt. Zero artificial additives. A product with a genuinely extraordinary story — and a digital presence that wasn't telling it.

When I conducted a full digital audit, the gap was clear: zero active paid campaigns, an Instagram following of just 1,038 against an industry average of 10,000–50,000, and two direct competitors already running active Meta campaigns targeting the same audiences. The brand had exceptional offline equity but was invisible where its customers were spending their time.

The strategy I developed focused on one central truth: the product is the story. Kerry cow milk, Dingle sea salt, Gaeltacht heritage — these aren't just ingredients, they're competitive advantages. The work was to turn that story into a structured, phased digital presence that could convert the brand's exceptional offline reputation into sustainable digital growth.

02 · Brand Audit

Where the brand
stands — honestly

Strengths

  • Premium artisan brand positioning
  • Rare Kerry cow milk — genuine differentiation
  • Zero artificial additives — full transparency
  • Prime tourist locations across Ireland
  • Strong Irish heritage and cultural storytelling
  • Energy efficiency +19% improvement (Bord Bia, 2024)

Weaknesses

  • Only 6 retail locations — limited scale
  • Heavy seasonal and tourist dependency
  • High cost structure from rare ingredients
  • Geographic concentration in Kerry
  • Zero paid digital advertising investment
  • Instagram following significantly below industry average

Opportunities

  • Artisanal market: $6.4B → $11.6B by 2035
  • Premium segment +18.4% luxury growth
  • National expansion through retail partnerships
  • Export via Irish diaspora and cultural heritage
  • Health and sustainability trends favour natural ingredients

Threats

  • Competitors with larger paid media budgets
  • Kerry cow milk supply chain vulnerabilities
  • Premium price sensitivity in economic downturns
  • Seasonal demand fluctuation and weather dependency
  • Consumer shift toward plant-based alternatives

03 · Strategy

Three pillars.
One story.

01

Bring the Magic Online

Behind-the-scenes craftsmanship, local ingredient sourcing, and staff stories — turned into a consistent video and Reels content strategy. The product makes itself; the job is to show it.

Tactics: Video series · Reels · Staff features · Flavour polls

02

Convert Tourists & Locals

Geo-targeted Meta and Google Ads designed around peak tourist windows. A Summer Passport loyalty programme. Flavour bundles and Reels ads targeting both repeat visitors and first-time tourists.

Target: +20% sales · +25% loyalty sign-ups · 3–5% ad CTR

03

Own the Irish Story

Ingredient spotlights. Food influencer collaborations in the travel and culture niche. Blog and email content that positions the brand as Ireland's definitive artisan ice cream — not just a product, but a place.

Target: Positive brand sentiment >80% · +50% organic mentions
