Chapter 01 Where the story begins

About

An engineer
who learned
to listen

I started with code. Two degrees in Computer Science gave me the ability to think in systems — to see how things connect, where they break, and how to make them faster. But somewhere along the way, I realised what I was most drawn to wasn't the architecture of software.

It was the architecture of attention.

How does a person find something they didn't know they were looking for? How does a brand earn trust in a search result, a scroll, a headline? That question pulled me into SEO and digital marketing — and I've spent five years finding the answer, one campaign at a time.

Today I work at the intersection of technical SEO, performance marketing, CRM, paid media, and content strategy. I've grown organic traffic by 55% for a global B2B company, led multi-brand campaigns across five verticals, and built a non-profit's digital presence from a wireframe up. I also wrote a book. Because some stories need more than a landing page.

Chapter 05 The one I wrote myself

The book

A reminder that
not fitting the mould
is a feature

Certified Misfit is a book about identity, empowerment, and non-conformity. It's about the people who don't slot neatly into job descriptions, org charts, or social expectations — and why that's worth something.

Writing it taught me what every good brand campaign needs: a clear point of view, a reader worth writing for, and the courage to say something that doesn't sound like everyone else. That's the same skill I bring to every content strategy I build.

"The people who don't fit the mould are often the most interesting ones in the room."
