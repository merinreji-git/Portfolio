Cullen Sheep Farm · Agri-tourism

Shaping a rural Irish farm into a memorable visitor story.

Cullen Sheep Farm already had the thing tourism brands work hard to invent: place, character, animals, landscape, and a story people could carry home. My work focused on shaping those raw strengths into digital initiatives, visual assets, outreach, content, and visitor-facing ideas.

  • Digital initiatives
  • Brand creatives
  • Online and offline marketing
  • Merchandise and souvenirs

The opportunity

A farm visit is not only an activity. It can become a memory.

For a rural Irish farm, the opportunity was not to make it feel overly polished or commercial. It was to help the experience become easier to discover, easier to understand, and easier for visitors to remember after they leave.

PlaceRural Irish farm with agri-tourism potential.
AudienceFamilies, tourists, local visitors, schools, and experience seekers.
ChallengeTranslate a real farm experience into clear digital and physical touchpoints.
DirectionBuild visibility, visitor interest, and brand memory without losing authenticity.

What I worked on

Digital ideas, printed pieces, outreach, and the small details visitors take home.

Digital initiatives

Started shaping a clearer digital presence for the farm: how the experience is introduced online, what visitors need to know first, and how the story can be carried through search, social, and website touchpoints.

Brand and digital creatives

Designed and directed creative assets that could make the farm feel more recognisable: brand visuals, digital creatives, social-friendly material, and design directions for visitor communication.

Outreach and visibility

Worked on outreach thinking for tourism, local discovery, partnerships, and ways to put the farm in front of people who are already looking for meaningful Irish experiences.

Printables and offline marketing

Planned printable and offline materials that could support the on-site experience: simple handouts, visitor prompts, information pieces, and physical reminders of the farm.

Content creation

Looked at how the farm can tell its story through animals, landscape, seasonal moments, behind-the-scenes activity, and the everyday charm of rural life.

Merchandise and memorabilia

Explored ideas for souvenirs, merchandise, and keepsakes that make the visit last longer: small, meaningful items people can buy, gift, or remember.

Strategy

Online discovery and offline memory needed to work together.

For this kind of project, digital marketing cannot stop at getting someone to click. The full experience matters: what they see before visiting, what they understand when they arrive, what they photograph, what they buy, and what they talk about afterwards.

The strategy was to treat Cullen Sheep Farm as both a place and a story: searchable enough for tourists, warm enough for families, visual enough for social media, and memorable enough to become part of someone’s trip.

Visitor touchpoints

The work stretched across the full journey.

Before the visit

Clearer digital creatives, searchable content ideas, tourism-friendly messaging, and outreach that helps people understand why the farm is worth visiting.

During the visit

Offline pieces, printables, signs, prompts, and brand moments that can make the experience easier to follow and more enjoyable to share.

After the visit

Souvenirs, memorabilia, social content, and visual reminders that help the farm stay in the visitor’s memory beyond the day itself.

For the brand

A more connected identity across digital and physical materials, so the farm feels recognisable wherever someone first meets it.

“Helping more people notice what was already special.”
