Aunua · Community · Social causes

Designing around a mission that exists to support people.

Aunua is rooted in community, inclusion, youth wellbeing, empowerment, and social causes. The work I do for Aunua is led by that mission first, whether it becomes a strategy piece, a digital creative, a website page, or a storybook.

The context

When the cause is human, the communication has to stay human too.

The work needed to move between audiences and formats while still feeling grounded in care: clear enough for a website, warm enough for community content, visual enough for campaigns, and imaginative enough for a storybook.

OrganisationAUNUA, a social-impact organisation focused on community, inclusion, and meaningful change.
AudienceYoung people, families, communities, partners, volunteers, and supporters.
ChallengeTranslate social causes into digital and creative touchpoints people can connect with.
DirectionKeep the work mission-led, human, accessible, visual, and easy to understand.

What I worked on

Mission-led strategy, digital creatives, website design, and story-led work.

Strategy pieces

Supported strategic thinking around how Aunua’s community-led message can be presented clearly across content, campaigns, digital pages, and public-facing materials.

Digital creatives

Created and shaped visual assets for social and digital use, keeping the focus on causes, people, and the warmth of a mission-led organisation.

Website design, wireframing & build

Designed the website from wireframe to development, shaping the page structure, visual direction, and build so Aunua’s purpose and programmes are easier to understand.

Storybook collaboration

Helped shape a storybook project where writing, visual mood, page flow, and AI-assisted design support came together around empathy and social impact.

AI-assisted visuals

Used AI as part of the creative process for visual exploration, mood direction, and early design routes, while keeping the strategy and message rooted in the cause.

Social-impact storytelling

Focused on making the message feel understandable and human, so the work supports the community story rather than simply decorating it.

Approach

From cause to communication.

The work with Aunua has not sat in one neat category because the mission itself touches many people and many needs. It has moved from planning to making, from copy to visuals, from wireframes to development, and from digital pages to story-led creative.

AI has made parts of the process easier, but the important work is still choosing the right strategy: a post that respects the cause, a website that guides the reader, a storybook that invites empathy, and visuals that help the mission feel alive.

Creative journey

The work moved through three mission-led layers.

Clarify

Make the mission and social causes easier to understand through structure, hierarchy, and simpler messaging.

Visualise

Turn community-focused ideas into posts, page concepts, storybook moods, and digital creative directions.

Connect

Create materials that feel warm enough for people to care and clear enough for them to take the mission seriously.

“The work became a way to turn social causes into something people could see, follow, and feel.”
